Kafagoz is where I keep my small experiments. Each one starts as a question and ends up as a page I can share.

A few of them: a four-key chord keyboard that types letters from finger patterns, a Catan board generator with pip and adjacency rules, a Splendor card explorer that shows what your gem hand can afford, and a free fall simulator that drops two balls off the Galata Tower.
